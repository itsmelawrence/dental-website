Your information matters

Privacy policy.

A plain-language starting point for how appointment enquiries are handled.

Template notice: This sample policy must be reviewed and adapted by the clinic before going live, including for compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Information we collect

When you request an appointment, we collect the name, mobile number, optional email, chosen service and dentist, preferred date and time, and any scheduling notes you provide. Please do not send medical or other sensitive information through this form.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry, coordinate a possible appointment, and answer related questions. Submitting the form does not confirm an appointment.

How the information is handled

The form sends an email to the clinic’s configured recipient. The template does not save enquiry details in a database. The clinic must secure its mailbox and any third-party form provider it chooses for a static export.

How long we keep it

Sample policy: appointment enquiries are retained for up to 12 months after the last contact, unless a longer period is required by law. Replace this with the clinic’s actual schedule.

Your choices and questions

You may ask to access or correct your information and raise a privacy concern. Contact privacy@example.com to exercise your rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. The clinic should provide its actual privacy contact and final procedures before publishing.
